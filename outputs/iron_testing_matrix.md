## Pro-Tip for Your Fitting Appointment

Focus your time on **Forged Cavity clubs in Tier A–B** (Ranks #11–#21). Your feel anchor — MP-54 + X-22 Tour — is a forged progressive cavity profile. The `Hollow (GI)` clubs (#9, #10) can be skipped entirely.

When testing, ask your fitter to pair with a lightweight, high-launch shaft:
- **Nippon N.S. Pro Zelos**
- **KBS Tour Lite**
- **Fujikura Axiom Graphite**

> **Note on Mizuno JPX 925 Forged (#15):** Closest living relative to the MP-54. Start here if you want a direct feel comparison with upgrade performance.
